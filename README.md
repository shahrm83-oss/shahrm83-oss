# Reeyan Shah

Mechanical engineering student at Boston University (class of 2029). I design, model and build energy systems and small vehicles.

**Portfolio:** [shahrm83-oss.github.io](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io)

## Projects
- [Sizing a 100 kWh/day hybrid solar system](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/solar.html): panel, battery and inverter selection, plus a 7-day hourly Excel simulation that cut wasted energy from over 60% to 8%
- [RC car for Terrier Motorsport](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/rc-car.html): designed in Fusion 360, built in 2 weeks on the CNC mill, lathe and 3D printer ([files](https://github.com/shahrm83-oss/TM-Car))
- [Four-bar rally-car linkage](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/four-bar.html): 13-item SolidWorks assembly with a two-sheet engineering drawing
- [Arduino temperature monitor](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/temperature-monitor.html): LCD readout with a non-blocking alarm
- [Salt corrosion in rooftop solar mounts](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/corrosion.html): team materials study ([summary](https://github.com/shahrm83-oss/pv-corrosion-study))
